Based on the reconciled dataset of 600 total bookings generating ₹10,47,973 in total revenue across six cities and six service categories, key operational and category insights emerged. From a city operations standpoint, Bengaluru recorded the highest Service Level Agreement (SLA) breach rate in the dataset at 15.9%, totaling 17 breaches across 107 bookings. This rate is 2.7 percentage points higher than the overall dataset average breach rate of 13.2% (79 total breaches), with Hyderabad following as the second highest at 14.3% (14 breaches across 98 bookings). From a category perspective, Deep Home Cleaning served as the primary driver of both volume and revenue, generating ₹5,08,964 across 176 bookings, whereas Salon for Women recorded the lowest volume with 65 bookings generating ₹1,51,689 in revenue. Consequently, the dashboard provides a centralized tool for City Ops to monitor and address SLA performance in high-breach locations while allowing Category Leads to track order volume and revenue performance across service lines.
